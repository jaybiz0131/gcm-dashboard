Family rollup

What the ecosystem earned, and how much of it came from one site handing a reader to another. The traffic dashboard answers who arrived; this answers what happened after they did.

Reading data/affiliate-rollup.json

Where these numbers come from. Cloudflare and Search Console cannot see an outbound click, because the click leaves for a network we do not run. So this page reads network exports instead: drop the CSVs in data/affiliates/drop/ and run python3 scripts/import_affiliates.py. The cross-site column is not a separate tracking system: the origin travels in the network's second click reference, so the handoff is reported back to us as part of the money data.
